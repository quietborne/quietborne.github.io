Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

Quietborne is a one-person apparel studio. We collect almost nothing, and this page explains exactly what "almost" means.

Who we are

Quietborne is an independent apparel brand making animal-metaphor graphic tees, operated by a single individual. You can reach us at quietborneshop@gmail.com. Where this policy says "we" or "us", it means that one person.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers or advertising pixels, has no accounts, and has no forms. Reading this page does not create a record of you anywhere we can see.

Our web host keeps ordinary server logs, as essentially all web hosts do. We do not access, analyse, or retain those logs ourselves.

If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it to reply to you and nothing else. It stays in our mail account until we clear it out. We do not sell it, share it, or add it to a mailing list.

If you buy from us

Quietborne sells through Etsy. Etsy handles the storefront, the payment, and the order data, and Etsy's own privacy policy governs that relationship, not this one. See Etsy's Privacy Policy.

Etsy passes us the information needed to fulfil an order, such as your name and shipping address. We use it to send you your order and to handle any problem with it. We do not sell it or share it with anyone beyond the printing and shipping providers who physically fulfil the order.

Our Pinterest integration

We run a private, internal tool that connects to the Pinterest API so we can manage our own Pinterest presence. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and it has no users other than us.

Specifically, that integration:

  • Authenticates through Pinterest OAuth to one account — our own.
  • Requests only the scopes it needs to read and write our own boards and pins, plus basic information about our own account.
  • Does not access other Pinterest users' boards, pins, or profiles.
  • Does not collect information about people who view, save, or click our pins. We see only the aggregate statistics Pinterest itself shows any account holder.
  • Does not build profiles, run ads, or perform any tracking of individuals.

Access tokens are stored locally on our own machine. They are never transmitted to a server we do not control, never shared with a third party, and never sold. Anything the integration retrieves from Pinterest is used solely to create and organise our own content.

We can revoke this access at any time from Pinterest's account settings, and we will if we stop using the tool. If you use Pinterest, you can review and manage your own data at any time in your Pinterest account settings.

What we never do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers. We do not use third-party trackers. We have no interest in collecting more than the small amount described above.

Children

Quietborne is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Email us and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Given how little we collect, the answer is usually that we hold nothing beyond an email thread. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; we will honour those requests at the same address.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, we will replace this page and update the effective date at the top. We will not make a change retroactive without saying so plainly.
